About Us

Sakthi MultiCare Clinic is a trusted multi-speciality healthcare center providing quality medical services with experienced doctors and patient-friendly care. Established in 2012, we have been serving families across Chennai with dedication and compassion.

Our clinic is equipped with modern diagnostic tools and a team of qualified specialists across General Medicine, Dental Care, Dermatology, Pediatrics, and Orthopedics. We believe every patient deserves respectful, affordable, and effective healthcare.
