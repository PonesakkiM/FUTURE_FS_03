Health Tips

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration supports kidney function, skin health, and energy levels.

🏃 Exercise Regularly

30 minutes of moderate exercise 5 days a week reduces the risk of heart disease, diabetes, and depression.

😴 Prioritize Sleep

Adults need 7–9 hours of quality sleep. Poor sleep is linked to obesity, heart disease, and weakened immunity.

🥗 Balanced Diet

Include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Limit processed foods, sugar, and saturated fats.

🩺 Regular Checkups

Annual health screenings can detect conditions early. Don't wait for symptoms — prevention is better than cure.

🧘 Manage Stress

Chronic stress harms your heart and immune system. Practice meditation, deep breathing, or yoga regularly.
